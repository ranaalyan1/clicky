why talk instead of type

5.4× faster than typing

people type at roughly 35 words per minute and speak at about 190. that gap is the whole point: hold a key, say the thing, and go back to what you were doing. dictation is cleaned up before it lands, so false starts and mid-sentence corrections never make it into your document.
